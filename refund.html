Refund Policy: 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

We offer a full, 100% money-back guarantee if you are not satisfied with the final design, provided we have had the opportunity to revise the work according to your feedback.

What Qualifies for a Refund

  • The request is made within 30 calendar days from the Date of Final File Delivery.
  • You have worked with our team to utilize the 2 rounds of free revisions included in your package.
  • The final design substantially failed to meet the specifications outlined in your original project brief.

Important: Intellectual Property Clause

If a full refund is processed, the client agrees to immediately and irrevocably forfeit all rights and ownership to the design concepts, final files, and associated intellectual property created by Fast Graphic Design. You may not use, display, or distribute the work in any format, publicly or privately. Failure to comply with this forfeiture will result in the immediate voiding of the refund, and payment for services rendered will be due.

Non-Refundable Items

  • Rush or Expedited Delivery Fees (these cover time already spent).
  • Custom work or scope changes beyond the original package/brief.
  • Any services where the final design files have been formally approved or have been used publicly by the client.

How to Request a Refund

Contact us at info@fastgraphicdesign.co.uk with your order number and a clear explanation of your concerns.

  • Our team will confirm that the revision process has been exhausted.
  • We will process the full refund to your original payment method within 5-7 business days of approval. Please allow your bank an additional 5-10 days for the credit to appear on your statement.

Before requesting a refund, we encourage you to work with us on revisions. Your package includes:

  • 2 rounds of free revisions
  • Direct communication with the design team
  • Multiple design concepts to choose from
